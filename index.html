ABOUT STUART

A writer who understands how words should sound.

I'm Stuart Carruthers, a writer and technical communicator with more than 20 years of experience working with software, technology and consumer products.

I'm also an author, which means I spend a lot of time thinking about rhythm, character, tone and the small details that make words connect with an audience.

I'm developing my voice-over and audiobook work with a particular interest in storytelling, children's books, comedy, character narration, technical content and conversational reads.
